Why it works from a bright sky

Emission nebulae do not glow across the spectrum. They emit at particular wavelengths, mainly hydrogen-alpha at 656.3nm and doubly-ionised oxygen at 500.7nm. Streetlighting is spread much more broadly. That asymmetry is the whole opportunity, and the L-eXtreme exploits it by opening two 7nm windows on those lines and closing everything else.

Look at how much of the bar is shut. The sodium line at 589nm, which is a large part of what a city throws upward, falls neatly in the gap between the two windows. From my balcony this is the difference between a target being possible and not, and it is why almost every emission nebula in the gallery was shot through it, including the Rosette, the Eagle, the Jellyfish, the Christmas Tree, Thor's Helmet, the Monkey Head and the Cat's Paw, the last of those from the balcony rather than the desert.

Living with it

Rejecting that much light means less of it arrives, so sub-exposures get longer and total integration has to grow. It is a trade of time for contrast, and from a bright site it is a trade well worth making. Star colour also largely disappears, since only two narrow slices of the spectrum are getting through, which is expected rather than a fault and is dealt with in processing.

7nm is the forgiving width. It tolerates faster optics better than a 3nm filter does, and it needs less integration to get there. If you are choosing a first dual narrowband filter, this is the one I would start with.

Where it will not help

Galaxies, star clusters and reflection nebulae shine by broadband starlight, so this filter throws away almost everything you came for. For those, the L-Quad Enhance is the right tool, and the Pleiades went unfiltered entirely.

Getting one

Filters are one of the few things in this hobby that genuinely do not wear out. There are no moving parts, no electronics and no consumables, so a coating in good condition performs exactly as it did new. That makes the used market a reasonable place to buy one: inspect for scratches and coating damage, and check it is the model and size you think it is. New, Optolong sell through the usual dealers and shipping to Dubai has not been a problem.
