The preview is a guide to scale and proportion, not a colour proof. Screens vary, and a print on paper or canvas will always look a little different from a backlit display. Images shown here carry the site watermark. The print you receive does not.

Choosing a size

  • Above a sofa, aim for artwork about two thirds the width of the sofa.
  • Hang the centre of the picture at roughly eye level, about 145 to 155 cm above the floor.
  • A corridor is seen from close up, so a smaller print holds its detail better there.
  • Canvas has a matte surface, which helps in a room with a lot of daylight.
  • Deep sky images carry a lot of fine detail, so bigger is usually better if the wall allows it.
