By daylight

It is also, incidentally, a lovely portrait lens — the rendering wide open is creamy and the sharpness is there when you want it. Manual focus at f/2 on a moving subject is a skill rather than a convenience, so this is not the lens for photographing children, but for anything that stays still it is excellent.

Buying one

Sold as Samyang in most of the world and Rokinon in North America; the lens is identical. It exists in every common mount, and because it is fully manual the adapted versions behave exactly like the native ones — there is no electronic communication to lose in the adapter. That makes it one of the few lenses genuinely worth buying in a different mount if the price is right. Used copies are common and, with no autofocus motor and no electronics to fail, there is very little that can be wrong with one that looks clean.
