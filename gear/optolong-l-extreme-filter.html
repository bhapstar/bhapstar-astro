Optolong L-eXtreme Filter

A dual narrowband filter passing 7nm bands at Hydrogen-alpha and Oxygen-III, and blocking nearly everything else, including most city skyglow. That is what makes emission nebulae workable from a Bortle 8 balcony. The trade is exposure time, because rejecting that much light means much longer sub-exposures.

Why it works from a bright sky

Emission nebulaeA cloud of gas that glows with its own light, in a few exact colours, because nearby stars are energising it. That asymmetry is the whole opportunity, and the L-eXtreme exploits it by opening two 7nm windows on those lines and closing everything else.

Look at how much of the bar is shut. The sodium line at 589nm, which is a large part of what a city throws upward, falls neatly in the gap between the two windows. From my balcony this is the difference between a target being possible and not, and it is why almost every emission nebula in the gallery was shot through it, including the Rosette, the Eagle, the Jellyfish, the Christmas Tree, Thor's Helmet, the Monkey Head and the Cat's Paw, the last of those from the balcony rather than the desert.

Living with it

Rejecting that much light means less of it arrives, so sub-exposuresOne single exposure out of the many that get stacked together. Sixty subs of one minute each make one hour of data. get longer and total integrationThe total time of every frame added together. Three hours of integration might be one hundred and eighty frames of one minute. has to grow. It is a trade of time for contrast, and from a bright site it is a trade well worth making. Star colour also largely disappears, since only two narrow slices of the spectrum are getting through, which is expected rather than a fault and is dealt with in processing.

7nm is the forgiving width. It tolerates faster optics better than a 3nm filter does, and it needs less integration to get there. Getting one

Filters are one of the few things in this hobby that genuinely do not wear out. There are no moving parts, no electronics and no consumables, so a coating in good condition performs exactly as it did new. That makes the used market a reasonable place to buy one: inspect for scratches and coating damage, and check it is the model and size you think it is. New, Optolong sell through the usual dealers and shipping to Dubai has not been a problem.
